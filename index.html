SEAT Leon (AF70YMC) Rejection Case

Case Summary

Will Holdsworth officially rejected SEAT Leon 1.5 FR (AF70YMC) on September 16 2025 at 9.36am, citing unsatisfactory quality under the Consumer Rights Act 2015. The rejection was based on non-functioning front/side parking sensors and a glitching cockpit screen.

Evidence: Image of non-functioning sensors (PXL_20250913_100916384.jpg), video of glitching cockpit screen (PXL_20250904_201118138~2.mp4).

Diagnostic Report From Independent Mechanic: Independent diagnostic report paid for by Will Holdsworth, conducted on September 13, 2025, at 3:55 PM. Organised when it had become clearer that Hilton were attempting to intentionally delay diagnostics beyond 30 days.

Contradictions/Bad Faith:

  • Claimed there was a power cut that prevented emailing me diagnostics during in-person meeting on September 16th 2025 at 9:30am - Contradicted by National Grid screenshot showing no power cuts lasting longer than 3 minutes for their postcode within the past 6 months (screencapture-powercuts-nationalgrid-co-uk-MK11BU-2025-09-17-08_32_24.png).
  • Verbal claimed in person at 9:30am September 16th that there are no faults with the vehicle. Claimed that the glitching cockpit screen was the map switching to night mode. After the rejection at 9:36am their follow up email used language that claimed there are no faults and a software update would resolve "all related codes". - Quote from Roy in email on September 16, 2025, at 6:25 PM: "The mechanics suggest that all codes are related to the software update" (Email). Contradicted by the Product Support Technician at Snap-On (manufacturer of diagnostic tool used by Hilton) on September 19, 2025, at 10:08 AM, confirming the diagnostic images sent by Hilton to Will Holdsworth at 6:25pm on September 16th 2025 indicate "faults within different modules which require attention" (Email).
  • Roy verbally claimed no knowledge of a booked diagnostic that Will Holdsworth attended on 30th August 2025 - Yellow keychain from initial diagnostic inspection was kept as evidence that shows what was diagnosed prior to second diagnostic inspection on 15th September 2025 (PXL_20250913_090149776.jpg).
  • Change of language - Roy mentions faults in email on September 15, 2025, at 4:04 PM: "looking into the fault for the sensors" (Email). Sent follow up after official rejection on September 16, 2025, at 6:25 PM, saying they would only pay for an "update" from a main dealer. No mention of attempting to cover the cost to repair faults. Language seemed intentionally vague and made no mention of repairing faults. (Email).
  • Claims I agreed to repairs in email on September 17, 2025, at 10:09 AM (Email) - Contradicted by rejection email on September 16, 2025, at 9:36 AM. Also contradicted by Hilton continuing to email diagnostic information to Will Holdsworth at 18:25pm on September 16th 2025 after the official rejection of the vehicle. (Email).
